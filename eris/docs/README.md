## Key changes in R10 vs. earlier revisions (R3)

- Program renamed "Expedited Research **Innovation System**" (was
  "Implementation Series").
- The enduring topic-area list changed substantially. R10 topics: (1) space
  operations in LEO/MEO/GEO and Cislunar (platform design, thermal
  management, thrusters, ISRU, microgravity manufacturing, AI/ML for space
  systems); (2) resilience/efficiency of strategic systems (critical
  infrastructure, C2 networks); (3) chem/bio defense of humans and the
  bioeconomy; (4) human health and performance / resilience across the
  deployment cycle, including AI/ML convergence with chemistry and biology.
  The earlier "elusive objects", "sensing limits", and "advanced antennas"
  topics no longer appear.
- New submission ceiling: only the first **three submissions per entity per
  monthly collection period** are reviewed (special-topic submissions do not
  count against the ceiling).

## Staying current

DARPA revises the master announcement periodically (R10 superseded R6, which
superseded R5, etc.) and old attachments are removed from SAM.gov. Before a
submission, re-check the notice above for a newer revision, and check
https://www.darpaconnect.us/eris for the live topic list and office hours.
